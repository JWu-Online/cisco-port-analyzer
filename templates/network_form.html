      How each switch's layer is decided
      Core
      Models that are usually core (Nexus 7000/9500, Catalyst 6500/6800/9600). With none, the switch linked to the most others. Routers and firewalls are drawn with the core.
      Distribution
      Models that are usually distribution (Catalyst 4500, 9400, 9500, Nexus 5000/3000), or a switch one step below the core with other switches below it.
      Access
      Models that are usually access (Catalyst 2960, 3560-3850, 9200, 9300, Dell PowerConnect, Aruba S3500), and everything at the edge.
      Your choice
      Click a switch on a map and pick its layer. It's saved and every map uses it from then on.
      Links
      CDP and LLDP neighbors (switches and routers only; phones and access points are left out). A switch on your list that nothing reports, often a Dell next to a Cisco that doesn't run LLDP, is found by its MAC address in the other switches' MAC tables and drawn with a dashed line.
