Which switches

A switch your switches see that isn't on the list is drawn too (dashed) if its address is in this range, like 10.0.0.0/16 or 10.0.x.x; separate several with commas. It isn't logged into. Leave it empty to draw only your list.

      How each switch's layer is decided
      Core / distribution
      The Nexus switches (and any Catalyst 6500/6800/9600). They do both jobs, so there's no separate distribution row. With no Nexus on the list, the switch linked to the most others.
      Access
      Everything else: the closets, grouped by building under the core switches they're plugged into.
      Your choice
      Click a switch on a map and pick its layer, for example Distribution for a switch that feeds other closets. It's saved and every map uses it from then on.
      Links
      CDP and LLDP neighbors that are switches: the ones on your list, plus any switch in the address range you give (drawn dashed, since it isn't logged into). Routers, servers, phones and access points are left out, and so is a switch outside the range; those switches are named at the top of the map, in case one should be on your list. A switch on your list that nothing reports, often a Dell next to a Cisco that doesn't run LLDP, is found by its MAC address in the other switches' MAC tables and drawn with a dashed line.
