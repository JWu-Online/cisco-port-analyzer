LLDP neighbors

What's plugged into each port: every phone, access point, switch and other device that tells the switch who it is (LLDP, and CDP on Cisco). See it switch by switch, and get CSVs.

Which switches

Uses the switch list from the Port Analyzer page. Each switch is asked for its ports and its LLDP neighbors (and CDP neighbors on Cisco). A PC usually doesn't send LLDP, so its port shows as "link up, nothing said who it is"; the MAC addresses on it are in Port documentation. See a made-up building

      What's in each CSV

      Devices: a row for every device found. Every port: a row for every port, with the device columns empty where nothing said who it is (and two rows for a port with two devices). One CSV per switch: every port, a file per switch.

      Switch, Address, Building, Location
      The switch's name (from its prompt) and what your switch list says about it.
      Port, Port description, Status, VLAN
      The port, its description on the switch, its status in the switch's own words (connected, notconnect, disabled, err-disabled...) and its VLAN (Cisco).
      Connected device, Device type
      The name the device gave (its IP or MAC address if it gave none), and Phone, Access point, Switch or router, or Other device (a printer, a camera, a server...).
      Device IP, Model / platform
      Its management address and what it says it is, like CP-8845 or C9120AXI-B.
      Its port, Capabilities, Device MAC
      Its own port that's plugged in, what it says it can do (B bridge, T telephone, W access point, R router...), and its MAC address when it sends one.
      Found by
      LLDP, CDP, or CDP + LLDP when the device said it both ways.
