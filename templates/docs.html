Port documentation

CSVs of every port on every switch in your list: switch, port, description, VLANs, use, power and the MAC addresses on it.

Which switches

Uses the switch list from the Port Analyzer page. Cisco Catalyst and Dell ports get everything the Stack topology page shows; Cisco Nexus and Aruba ports get the same columns where those switches have the data.

Log in and make the CSVs

Passwords are only kept in memory for this run, never saved.

      What's in each row
      Switch, Address, Building, Location
      The switch's name (from its prompt) and what your switch list says about it.
      Member/slot, Port, Interface
      Stack member (or chassis slot), the port number on it, and the full port name like Gi2/0/14.
      Description
      The port's description on the switch.
      VLAN, VLAN name
      The port's VLAN (on a Dell, its PVID), or Trunk.
      Tagged VLANs
      Other VLANs on the port: the voice VLAN, or the VLANs a trunk carries ("all" if it isn't limited).
      Status, Speed, Duplex
      Link up, no link, shut down or err-disabled, and what it linked at.
      Result, Last active, Why
      Active, Inactive or Unsure for the months you picked, the same way as the Stack topology page.
      Issues, Power
      Power and slow-connection problems, and what the port powers (Cisco Catalyst and Dell).
      MAC addresses, MAC count
      Every MAC address the switch has learned on the port, as 0011.2233.4455, and how many there are. Trunk ports just say "Trunk port", with no count.
